I'm sure there a lot of standards for how to write electrical schematics that I am unaware of. 
### 5/21/26
I have discovered net power labels. Or at least discovered their utility in this project. Basically, they are labels you attach to connections that say "everything with this label is connected." This is extremely useful for ground and constant voltages. It drastically decreases the amount of wires I need to use which increases the readability of the schematic. When I first began designing the schematic, I liked the idea of written connections between every pin. In my head I saw a beautiful diagram of wires and connections. However attempting this was a reality check; physical connections between every pin without messy overlaps and poor readability is not realistic.
